# Reviews in Simplified Chinese (文献综述)

## Register

- Use written academic Chinese (书面语): 本文、研究表明、综上所述、值得注意的是.
  Avoid colloquial words (挺、特别多) and translationese (被…所…, long
  chains of 的, 进行了…的研究 where 研究了 suffices).
- Prefer clear, short sentences; one point per paragraph, with the topic
  sentence first (主题句).
- The usual structure: 引言、文献检索与筛选方法、各主题综述、讨论（研究不足与展望）、结论.

## Terms and names

- Translate technical terms with the established Chinese term and give the
  English in parentheses at first mention: 自由间接引语（free indirect
  discourse）. Keep one translation throughout.
- Foreign authors: keep the Latin spelling, family name only in running
  text: Smith [P4] 指出 …; or translate with the original at first mention:
  史密斯（Smith）[P4].
- Paper titles stay in their original language.

## Citations

- Place the citation before the sentence's closing punctuation:
  …存在显著差异 [P3, P7]。 The app turns them into GB/T 7714 numbers
  ([1], [2-4]) or the chosen style.
- Numbers, units and Latin letters are half-width; Chinese punctuation is
  full-width （，。；：“”）.

## Length

- Chinese reviews count characters: an abstract of 300-400 characters, theme
  sections of 1500-3000 characters each are typical for a journal review.

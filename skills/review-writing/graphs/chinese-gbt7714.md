# A review in Simplified Chinese with GB/T 7714 references

Use this graph to write a **literature review in Simplified Chinese** for a
Chinese journal, a thesis chapter (学位论文文献综述) or a research proposal
(开题报告), with references in GB/T 7714-2015 (the request's style is
`gbt7714` and its language `zh-CN`). The sources may be in any language;
most will often be in English.

Writing in Chinese about mostly English literature needs consistent
terminology and the register of Chinese academic writing, so the graph:

1. **glossary** - builds a glossary of the review's key terms: the
   established Chinese translation for each English term (with the English
   at first mention) and how author names are given;
2. **draft** - writes every section of the outline in Chinese academic
   style, citing by library id before the closing punctuation;
3. **polish** - rereads each section and revises register, terminology
   (against the glossary), paragraph structure and translationese, and makes
   sure each theme paragraph synthesizes several sources.

It writes the sections and returns the glossary and what was written and
revised. The abstract is left to the final review stage.

# A fast-moving computer science field

Use this graph to organize a review of a **computer science field that moves
in months**, where much of the work is on arXiv before or instead of peer
review: large language models and agents, diffusion models, retrieval-
augmented generation, graph neural networks, and similar.

In such fields a thematic structure goes out of date quickly and results are
hard to compare, so the graph:

1. **timeline** - orders the included papers by year, marks preprints and
   published papers, and names the milestones that changed the field;
2. **taxonomy** - groups the papers into method families (by core idea, not
   by application) and tags each paper with them;
3. **compare** - builds a comparison of the method families: core idea,
   typical benchmarks and data, reported strengths, limitations,
   representative papers - noting where results are not comparable
   (different benchmarks, settings, model sizes);
4. **gaps** - open problems, evaluation and reproducibility issues, what
   changes fastest, and cautions about relying on preprints.

It tags the papers with their method families and returns the timeline, the
taxonomy, the comparison and the gaps.

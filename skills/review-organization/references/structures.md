# Structures for a review

| Structure | When | Sections look like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thematic | Most topics; several aspects of one phenomenon | "Irony and narrative voice", "Irony and gender" |
| Methodological | The approach shapes the findings (lab vs field studies, models) | "Experimental studies", "Corpus studies" |
| Chronological | The field changed in phases | "Early work (1990-2005)", "The digital turn" |
| Theoretical | Competing frameworks explain the same thing | "Cognitive accounts", "Sociocultural accounts" |
| Debate | The field argues about a question | "Arguments for X", "Critiques", "Attempts to reconcile" |

Mixed structures are common: themes as sections, chronology inside each.

Good theme names say what the papers are about ("Feedback timing"), not that
they are papers ("Studies on feedback").

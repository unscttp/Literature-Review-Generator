# Comparing papers

- **Comparison table.** Rows are papers or groups, columns are the dimensions
  that matter for the research questions (data, method, setting, main
  finding, limitation). Empty cells show what is not reported.
- **Agreement.** Several papers with different data reaching the same result
  is strong evidence; say so with all their ids.
- **Contradiction.** Look for the reason: different populations, periods,
  definitions, measures or methods. Name it if the records show it,
  otherwise say the reason is unclear.
- **Weight.** A large study, a replication or a highly cited book weighs more
  than a small pilot or an unpublished preprint. Say what the weight rests on.
- **Gaps.** Missing populations, settings, languages, methods, periods or
  questions; contradictions nobody resolved.

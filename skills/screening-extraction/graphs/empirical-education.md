# Empirical education research

Use this graph to screen and extract in a review of **empirical studies in
education**: teaching interventions, learning outcomes, classroom practice,
educational technology, assessment, teacher education - quantitative,
qualitative or mixed-methods studies of learners, teachers or schools.

Such reviews are judged on transparent criteria and comparable evidence, so
the graph:

1. **criteria** - turns the scope into PICOS criteria with exclusion codes:
   Population (learners, level, setting), Intervention or phenomenon,
   Comparison, Outcomes, Study design (empirical only, unless the scope
   says otherwise);
2. **title-abstract** - screens every candidate on title and abstract,
   with the code in each reason, and leaves unclear cases as candidates;
3. **borderline** - reads the full records of the unclear cases and decides
   them;
4. **extract** - records for each included study the design, participants
   (number, level, country), intervention, outcome measures, main result
   (direction and effect size when reported) and limitations.

It returns the criteria, the screening counts per exclusion code and the
extraction summary.

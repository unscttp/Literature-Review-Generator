# Extracting evidence

## What good notes look like

- `method`: "Quasi-experiment, two classes (treatment and control), pre- and
  post-test." - not "an empirical study".
- `data`: "312 Year 8 students in 6 schools, Norway, 2019-2020."
- `findings`: "Reading comprehension improved more in the treatment group
  (d = 0.31); no difference in motivation." Report effect sizes, numbers and
  directions when the abstract gives them.
- `limitations`: "Self-reported outcomes; no long-term follow-up."

## Rules

- Only from the record. If the abstract does not report the sample, write
  `not reported`.
- Keep the same fields comparable across papers: same units, same terms.
- Notes are written in the review language; quote key terms in the original
  where translation would lose meaning.
- Read up to 8 records per `library_get` and save the notes of up to 10 papers per
  `library_note` call: a review of many papers then needs few calls.
